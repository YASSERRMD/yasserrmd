## About

Solution Architect at Sharjah City Municipality with 19+ years of experience building large-scale systems.

Work focuses on designing production-grade AI platforms where performance, control, and privacy are core architectural constraints rather than afterthoughts.

> AI is a component of the system, not the system itself.

> Privacy is enforced through design, not policy.
