## About Me

Solution Architect at Sharjah City Municipality with over 19 years of experience building large-scale software platforms and government solutions.

Current focus areas:
- AI infrastructure and applied LLM systems
- edge and browser-native AI
- small language models for practical deployment
- Rust-based high-performance systems
- retrieval pipelines and vector architectures

> Small models with strong architecture outperform larger systems without structure.

> Privacy should be enforced by system design.

> AI should serve the system, not replace architectural thinking.
