## About Me 

Solution Architect at Sharjah City Municipality with 19+ years of experience designing and delivering large-scale software solutions for government environments.

Current work sits at the intersection of AI infrastructure, edge-first deployment, retrieval systems, and high-performance engineering. The focus is not on AI as a demo layer, but on building systems that remain reliable under real operational constraints such as privacy, latency, cost, and control.

## What Defines My Work

- Small language models for practical deployment
- Privacy-first system design
- Rust-based high-performance data planes
- Browser-native and edge AI architectures
- Retrieval systems as part of system architecture
- Fine-tuned domain models for real-world use cases
- Production-oriented AI engineering over hype-driven prototypes

## Selected Systems & Open Source Work

### barq-web-rag
Browser-native retrieval system designed for local-first usage. Documents are processed and searched without relying on a traditional backend-centric workflow.

### barq-vweb
Vector database built for WebAssembly environments with local persistence and semantic retrieval capabilities.

### barq-wasm
High-performance Rust compute layer focused on vector-heavy workloads and efficient browser execution.

### AiMesh
Control-oriented infrastructure for AI workloads, handling orchestration, routing, and execution flow across components.

### Domain-focused model work
Practical work on small language models, fine-tuning, quantization, distillation, and domain-specific adaptation for production constraints rather than benchmark theater.
